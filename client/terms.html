Terms and Privacy Policy

Rules of use

  • Use TaskFlow only for lawful work and study projects.
  • Keep your password private and do not share your account.
  • Do not upload harmful, abusive or illegal content.
  • We may remove accounts that break these rules.

Your data

  • We store your name, email and an encrypted (hashed) password, plus the projects and tasks you create.
  • Your data is used only to run TaskFlow. We do not sell it.
  • Project members can see the projects and tasks they are added to.
  • You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time.

TaskFlow is a learning project built for the CodeAlpha internship.
